# Reference assets

Internal design references for Mission 001. Source: **Fitlite, Fitness Coach &
Classes by Neoteric** on Dribbble, https://dribbble.com/shots/18860790-Fitlite-fitness-coach-classes-by-Neoteric

These images are analysis material, not product assets. Do not ship them in the
app, and do not reuse their branding, logo, Latin copy, or fitness imagery.

## What the reference contributes

- Light-first composition with white surfaces, thin borders, and almost no shadow.
- One saturated color block per card, with dark ink text over it.
- Oversized, tightly set display headings against small uppercase labels.
- Pill-shaped black CTAs and pill tags for metadata.
- A segmented control for view filtering (All / In studio / Online).
- A two-column desktop layout: content grid plus a narrow profile sidebar.
- A centered circular portrait, name, rating, and specialty tags on the profile.
- Booking and success dialogs as focused, centered cards with a colored summary block.
- A strict visual rhythm: date group headings, card rows, generous whitespace.

## Adaptation rules for Shena

- Keep the composition and rhythm; replace the fitness subject matter with
  swimming pools, instructors, lessons, and water.
- Keep the colorblock device but limit the accent set to water-adjacent tints
  derived from the Shena palette. One accent per card, never a full background.
- Replace the condensed Latin display face with Vazirmatn at heavy weights.
  Persian has no direct equivalent of a condensed display face, so scale and
  weight carry the hierarchy instead.
- Mirror every composition for RTL; flip directional icons, keep photography and
  faces unmirrored.
- Drop the reference's black brand pills where they read as fitness branding;
  use deep navy for primary actions and aqua for emphasis.
- The reference is inspiration for structure and confidence, not a template to
  reproduce pixel for pixel.
